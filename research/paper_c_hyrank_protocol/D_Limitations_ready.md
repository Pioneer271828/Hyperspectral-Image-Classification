The admitted observations are previously exposed development material from one original product per scene. Class-balanced K5/V5 sampling describes the released sample rather than the natural class prevalence of a full scene. Model seeds, reversed directions, multiple methods and repeated predictions do not create new geographic or acquisition replicates. Disjoint parents and a four-pixel observation-box gap constrain access but do not establish spatial independence. This limitation is particularly direct in HongHu, where each common class has only one validation parent. Resampling the two observed parents can describe sensitivity; it cannot supply a calibrated population confidence interval. The three initialization seeds quantify model variation on fixed observations rather than uncertainty over sensor families.

Geography, acquisition date, platform, spatial resolution, class composition and preprocessing change together with the instrument family. Equal patch dimensions therefore do not imply equal ground support, and a transfer difference cannot be attributed solely to the sensor. The coarse ground and canopy groups retain differences in material and annotation granularity. Xuzhou's publisher-to-local conversion remains an explicitly corroborated inference. Historical label-count access and development validation exposure also prevent these roles from being described as untouched confirmation data.

Project raw values are processed product values, not uncalibrated sensor measurements. Approximate band centers do not authenticate native detector indices, response curves or a common physical spectral grid. Using target-scene population statistics is a distinct access condition and cannot be reported as target-unseen source-only inference. Missing response measurements alone do not invalidate a classification comparison, but they limit physical-response claims.

The proposed Hyperion–HySpex extension remains conditional on original class-value, product-processing and spatial-role records. A CLC-derived forest annotation is not equivalent to an individual tree crown, and a generic water label has not been bound to the Xuzhou lake definition. Different standard and derived radiometric stages must also be distinguished. No new Hyperion images, ground-truth arrays, parcel lists or trained endpoints have been admitted for that extension; independent confirmation and native VCA efficacy remain untested.
